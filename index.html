R2D2

A personal study assistant (non-commercial, single user).

What it is

R2D2 is a small personal assistant that runs on its developer's own computer and is used through a private Telegram chat. It helps a high-school student organise schoolwork: summarising class notes, tracking homework and answering questions about the school timetable.

How it uses Google services

With the explicit permission of the account owner (the developer himself), R2D2 connects to his own Google account in order to:

  • read his Google Calendar events and create events after he confirms each one;
  • read his Gmail messages and create email drafts.

R2D2 cannot send or delete emails. It only requests the minimum permissions needed for these features. The app is used by one person only: its developer.
