Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

R2D2 is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual developer. It is used only by its developer, on his own Google account and his own computer. It has no other users and no website accounts.

Google user data we access

  • Google Calendar: event titles, times, locations and descriptions (read), and creation of new events after explicit confirmation by the account owner.
  • Gmail: email messages (read) and creation of email drafts. R2D2 cannot send or delete emails.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer requests that the account owner makes himself, such as "what is on my calendar this week?" or "summarise my unread emails". It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

Where the data goes

  • Local storage: the Google sign-in token is stored in a file on the developer's own computer. R2D2 has no server and no database of its own, and does not keep copies of calendar or email content beyond the current conversation.
  • AI model: to produce an answer, the relevant calendar or email content is sent to Google's Gemini API, and is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy for that service.
  • Telegram: answers are delivered to the account owner through a private Telegram chat, subject to Telegram's own privacy policy.

Data is not shared with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

The account owner can revoke R2D2's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the local sign-in token file from his computer.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change. For questions, open an issue in the GitHub repository that hosts this site.
